Lambo Garage AI
The portfolio preview is sourced from Dhruvil Gohil's independent 3D showroom. The portfolio styles crop the image and apply a cool tint.
https://github.com/Dg-programmer/Lambo-garage-3d-ai

The showroom's exterior and cabin are adapted from Lamborghini Huracan STO 2020 by Raakesh.Madan under CC BY 4.0. Materials were adjusted and the mesh was divided into inspectable parts.
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/lamborghini-huracan-sto-2020-1d10481ff96246d4b67242bddd394d43
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The showroom's brake cutaway is adapted from Brake Disk Sport Carbon Ceramic by the 86 guy under CC BY 4.0. The model was rescaled and its markings and finish were changed. Both models were sourced through Ai2 Objaverse.
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/brake-disk-sport-carbon-ceramic-89de11361d504f479e5cb2df427699ab
https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse
